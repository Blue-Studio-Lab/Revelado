# Rescue mode (coming)

*[Español](../../es/manual/rescate.md)*

Rescue mode will help with frames that the normal pipeline cannot save on its own: severe underexposure, mixed light, strong colour casts, motion blur at the edge of acceptable.

How it is planned to work:

- Revelado proposes a few alternative recipes and shows them side by side.
- With AI enabled, Claude can propose and compare variants. There is a per-photo budget cap, and every value is clamped to safe ranges.
- You always choose, and nothing is applied silently.

Status: design stage, planned for v0.4. Follow the roadmap in the README.
